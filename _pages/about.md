I’m a computer science undergraduate at UC San Diego, expecting to graduate in December 2026. My background combines software engineering, AI research, business operations, and entrepreneurship.

I build practical tools with Python, SQL, FastAPI, PostgreSQL, React, and TypeScript. In Agent OS, I integrated Amazon operations data and developed data-quality checks and decision-support tools. My coursework covers data structures, algorithms, databases, scalable analytics, computer vision, and deep learning. I implemented configurable ResNet models in PyTorch and contributed an IndexedDB data layer to a team software project.

In UCSD’s Early Research Scholars Program, I developed Blender APIs and worked on model evaluation. I now tutor CSE110 Software Engineering.

Co-founding an e-commerce business and working in industry gave me experience communicating with customers, coordinating across teams, and connecting technical implementation with real business needs.
